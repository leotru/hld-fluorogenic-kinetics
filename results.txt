The published relative errors (Km 44%, KSI 43%) are reproduced at
about 15-16% proportional noise with 11 concentrations in triplicate,
or at lower noise if fewer concentrations were actually used.  The
correlation r(lnKm,lnKSI) = -0.97 is unchanged across every noise
level and every design tested: it is structural, not a data problem.

and the peak position.  Km and KSI individually are not, and they
should be quoted as a correlated pair, not as independent numbers.
